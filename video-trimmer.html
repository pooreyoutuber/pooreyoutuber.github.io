How to Use Online Video Trim Free

Online Video Trim Free lets you cut a clip out of a video without installing software, creating an account, or sending your file to a server. Everything you need is on this page. The steps below walk you through the process from upload to download.

Step-by-step guide

  1. Upload your video. Click “Choose Video” or drag your file onto the upload area. MP4, WebM and MOV files are accepted when your browser can play them. The file is read directly from your device.
  2. Check the details. Once the video loads, the file name, length, file size, resolution and format appear above the player.
  3. Find the part you want. Play the video or click anywhere on the timeline to jump to that moment. The light blue range shows the clip you will keep.
  4. Set the start and end points with the timeline. Drag the left handle to choose where the clip begins and the right handle to choose where it ends. On a phone, touch and drag the handles with your finger.
  5. Enter exact times. For precision, type times into the Start Time and End Time fields. Use MM:SS for clips under an hour and HH:MM:SS for longer videos. The tool rejects a start time that is not earlier than the end time, and an end time that goes past the video length.
  6. Use Set Start and Set End. Pause the video at the exact frame you want, then press Set Start or Set End. This is the fastest way to mark points while you watch.
  7. Preview the selection. Press “Preview selection” to play only the chosen part. Adjust the handles or the time fields if anything looks off.
  8. Trim the video. Press “Trim Video.” The first time you trim, your browser loads the processing engine. Only the selected section is then encoded.
  9. Wait for browser processing. A progress bar and status messages appear while the clip is created. Keep the tab open until processing finishes. You can cancel before the result is ready.
  10. Download the result. Press “Download Video” to save an MP4 file. It is named after your original file with “-trimmed” added.

Why trim videos in the browser?

Traditional editing programs need to be downloaded, installed and learned before you can make a simple cut. A browser-based trimmer skips all of that. You open the page, choose a file and start working, whether you are on a laptop, a tablet or a phone. There is no account to create and no subscription to manage.

Privacy is another benefit. Because the trimming runs on your device, your video is not uploaded to our servers for editing. The only network activity is downloading the processing engine itself, which happens once and can be cached by your browser.

Good uses for a quick trim

  • Cutting highlights from long videos for social media posts
  • Preparing clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and other short-form platforms
  • Shortening WhatsApp videos before sharing them with family or colleagues
  • Keeping only the relevant section of a tutorial or screen recording
  • Extracting one part of a recorded presentation or meeting
  • Trimming personal videos, such as the best moments from a family event

What affects processing speed?

Processing time depends on your device, the resolution and codec of the video, the length of the section you keep, and the performance of your browser. A high-resolution clip, such as 4K footage, needs more memory and more time than a short 720p clip. Older phones and low-memory laptops will take longer, while a recent computer usually finishes faster. Browser processing is not instant, and it is generally slower than desktop software that uses dedicated hardware acceleration. Large files may also need more memory, which is why the tool accepts files up to 500 MB.

Tips for better results

  • Close other heavy tabs and apps before trimming large files.
  • Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  • If you only need a short clip, select a short range. Shorter sections finish faster.
  • Keep the tab open and in the foreground while processing runs.
  • Plug in your laptop or phone so the device does not slow down to save power.
  • Use Preview selection before trimming to confirm the start and end points.
  • Remember that the output is re-encoded as an MP4 (H.264 video and AAC audio). This keeps the file widely compatible, but it can slightly change quality compared with the original.

Important limits to know

Trimming happens entirely on your device, so the speed and success of each job depend on your hardware. Very large or unusual files may fail, and the tool will tell you when that happens. Always check the preview in the result card before you download. If something looks wrong, trim again with a different range.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Online Video Trim Free?

It is a free online tool that lets you cut a section out of a video directly in your web browser. You choose a start and end point, trim the clip, and download the result as an MP4 file.

Do I need to install software?

No. The editor runs in your browser. The engine loads locally or from reliable public CDNs on demand.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Your video is read from your device and trimmed locally in your browser. It is not sent to any server operated by this tool.

Can I trim a video by entering exact times?

Yes. Type a start time and an end time in the fields provided, using MM:SS or HH:MM:SS. Decimal values are accepted.

What video formats are supported?

The tool accepts MP4, WebM, MOV and M4V files. The output is always a widely-compatible MP4 file.

Can I use this tool on mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones and tablets, and the timeline handles can be dragged with touch.

Why does processing take longer for large videos?

Larger and higher-resolution videos contain much more data to decode and encode. Browser processing uses your device's CPU and memory.

Where is my trimmed video stored?

The trimmed video is held in memory in your current browser tab until you download it or leave the page.
