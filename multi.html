YouTube Multi Viewer – Watch Multiple Videos Side by Side

Open multiple YouTube videos or Shorts simultaneously. Easily preview layouts, test video playback, and compare content performance across multi-screen windows directly in your browser.

💡 Usage Note: Designed for testing YouTube embeds, evaluating video responsiveness, and inspecting playback layout behavior across multiple player viewports.

How to Use the YouTube Multi Viewer Tool

Tool Overview: The YouTube Multi Viewer works entirely in your browser. It renders standard HTML5 YouTube iframe embeds locally without sending your video data to any external server.

The YouTube Multi Viewer by PooreYoutuber provides a clean and simple interface for creators and web developers to test video embedding and preview multiple video streams concurrently on desktop or mobile screens.

Instructions:

  • Step 1: Copy the URL of any YouTube video or YouTube Shorts.
  • Step 2: Paste the link into the input box above.
  • Step 3: Choose how many player screens you wish to open (e.g., 2, 4, 8, or 12).
  • Step 4: Select your preferred audio and autoplay configuration.
  • Step 5: Click Generate Players to display all video viewports side by side.

Why Use a Multi Video Player?

Testing video embeds across different grid aspect ratios is essential when designing media-centric websites or assessing video presentation. This browser viewer lets you quickly evaluate how video streams initialize, check player controls, and compare visual presentation without opening separate browser tabs.
