    About The Website & Payment Gateway Audit Tool

    Launching an e-commerce platform or digital business requires a solid technical foundation. This free, browser-based Audit Tool allows merchant site owners to run an instant analysis of their website’s technical SEO performance, security protocols, and payment gateway compliance readiness without downloading third-party software.

    How to Use the Audit Tool

    Using this tool is straightforward and requires zero technical expertise:

    1. Enter your full URL: Copy and paste your website URL (e.g., https://yourdomain.com) into the input box above.
    2. Click "Run Audit": The engine checks your domain structure, protocol security, page availability, and key policy paths.
    3. Review Your Score & Report: Within seconds, you will receive an overall score out of 100 alongside itemized diagnostics for technical health and policy readiness.
    4. Implement Recommendations: Follow the generated checklist under "Recommended Action Items" to address security gaps or missing compliance documents.

    Key Features Audited

    • SSL Certificate (HTTPS): Verifies secure encryption. Payment processors (like Stripe, PayPal, or Razorpay) strictly reject unencrypted sites to protect consumer credit card credentials.
    • Mobile Responsiveness: Ensures layout adaptiveness for smartphone users, directly impacting Google rankings and mobile conversion rates.
    • Estimated Speed Metrics: Evaluates simulated loading performance. Slow sites lead to cart abandonment.
    • Payment Compliance Pages: Checks for active mandatory legal documents (Privacy Policy and Refund/Cancellation Policy) essential for passing acquiring-bank merchant approvals.

    Benefits of Regular Site Audits

    Conducting routine site checks provides significant operational advantages:

    • Faster Merchant Onboarding: Payment gateway providers automatically decline applications missing required policy documents. Identifying missing pages beforehand prevents merchant account rejection.
    • Enhanced Trust & Security: Visible HTTPS protocols reassure customers that their transaction details are protected against interception.
    • Optimized Search Visibility: Search engine crawlers prioritize secure, fast, and mobile-friendly websites, boosting organic search placement.
    • Reduced Cart Abandonment: Swift loading times and clear checkout channels minimize friction during transaction completion.
