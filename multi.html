Important Notice & Ad Network Policy Compliance:

Website SEO Analytics Helper is a website testing utility that helps verify whether your website loads correctly and whether your Google Analytics 4 (GA4) implementation records page views after simulated browser visits. The tool opens your website a limited number of times in a controlled environment so you can confirm that your analytics setup is working as expected. It is intended for testing and debugging website analytics only. It is not designed to generate advertising revenue, manipulate analytics, improve search rankings, or bypass the policies of advertising or analytics platforms. Always follow the terms and policies of the services you use. Important: Do not use it repeatedly with the intention of generating ad impressions, increasing advertising revenue, or influencing analytics metrics. If your website displays advertisements, consider disabling them during testing.

How It Works

  1. Enter Test Keyword: Type the keyword context related to your content audit.
  2. Provide Target URL: Submit your primary website URL (up to 3 additional secondary URLs supported).
  3. Execute Audit: Click 'Start Verification Process' to trigger controlled viewport checks.
  4. Verify Analytics: Open Google Analytics realtime reports to verify incoming tracking hits.
