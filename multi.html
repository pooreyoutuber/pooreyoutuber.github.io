Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will my generated QR codes expire?

No! The QR codes generated by this tool are static and permanent. Because the data is hardcoded straight into the matrix patterns, your QR codes will never expire or stop functioning.

What file sizes are recommended for file-to-QR conversion?

For best scanning results across all smartphone cameras, we recommend uploading lightweight files (under 2MB–3MB). Larger files result in denser QR code patterns that require higher camera resolution to scan clearly.
