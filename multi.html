What Is This Free Online Video Trimmer?

This free online video trimmer lets you cut a video down to the exact part you need, directly inside your web browser. You do not have to download heavy software, create an account, or pay for a subscription. Simply open the page, load your video, select where the clip should begin and end, and download the result. Whether you want to remove a long intro, keep only the best moment of a recording, or shorten a clip for social media, this tool gives you precise control over every second.

How to Trim a Video Online: Step by Step

  1. Load your video. Drag the file into the box or click "Choose video file". You can select videos up to 20GB.
  2. Find the part you want. Play the video and pause where you want the clip to start, then press "Use current" next to the start time. Do the same for the end time.
  3. Fine-tune the selection. Move the blue handles on the timeline, or type an exact time such as 00:01:30.500 into the start or end box for frame-level accuracy.
  4. Preview. Press "Preview selection" to play only the part you chose. Adjust again if needed.
  5. Choose a quality level. High keeps the most detail and produces a larger file. Small file produces a much smaller download that is suitable for messaging apps.
  6. Trim and download. Press "Trim and Download". When the process finishes, your trimmed video is saved automatically to your device.

Why Trim Videos in Your Browser?

Many video editors are large programs that take time to install, need powerful hardware, and often require paid licenses for basic tasks. A browser-based trimmer solves a simple problem quickly. If you only need to cut the beginning or the end of a clip, a full editor is unnecessary. Browser tools are also available on any computer or phone you already own, so you can trim a video at work, at home, or on the go without installing anything.

Common uses include shortening screen recordings for tutorials, removing dead air from phone videos, cutting highlights from a sports or event recording, preparing short clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels or WhatsApp status, and extracting a single scene from a longer film for personal reference. Students, teachers, content creators, small business owners and everyday users all benefit from a fast and simple way to edit video.

Privacy and Security

Privacy is a core design principle of this tool. When you choose a file, the browser reads it from your own disk and plays it locally. The trimming process uses browser features that create the new clip on your device. Your original video and the finished result are never uploaded to any external server, which means personal, family, business or confidential footage stays under your control. Because no data leaves your computer, you also avoid the waiting time of uploading large files over the internet.

Tips for the Best Results

  • Keep the tab open and active. Trimming runs in real time, so a 5-minute selection takes about 5 minutes to export. Do not close the tab or put your computer to sleep during this time.
  • Use a desktop browser for very large files. Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox on a computer handle big files and recording features most reliably.
  • Close other heavy programs. Freeing up memory helps smooth playback and a clean export, especially with 4K or long videos.
  • Use exact times for precision. Typing a time in the boxes is faster and more accurate than dragging the slider for a frame-perfect cut.
  • Check the preview. Always play your selection before downloading. It takes seconds and prevents mistakes.

Important Limitations

This tool trims videos but does not add effects, text, music, transitions or filters. The output is re-encoded in the format your browser supports, usually WebM or MP4, so the file type may differ slightly from the original. Videos in formats that your browser cannot play, such as some MKV or HEVC files on certain devices, may not load. If that happens, try converting the file to MP4 first. Exported quality depends on the chosen quality level and on the source video.
