How to Play

1. Drop & Explore: You spawn into an island populated by 24 AI opponents. Search towns, military bases, and farms for loot.

2. Gear Up: Pick up weapons (Assault Rifles, Shotguns, Snipers), ammo, armor, and medkits scattered across the map.

3. Survive the Zone: Stay inside the shrinking circular safe zone visible on your minimap. The blue storm damages anyone outside.

4. Outlast Everyone: Eliminate enemies or let them fight each other. Be the last survivor standing to claim Victory Royale!
