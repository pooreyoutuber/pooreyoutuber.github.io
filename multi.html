How to Get the Most from Your YouTube Descriptions

A strong description helps viewers and the YouTube algorithm understand what your video is about. Use the generated text as a starting point, then personalize it with your own links, timestamps, and calls to action.

Best practices

Put the most important keywords near the beginning of the description. Add clear chapter timestamps if your video has sections. Include links to related videos, playlists, or resources. End with a friendly invitation to subscribe or comment. Avoid keyword stuffing — natural language performs better.

Length matters

Descriptions around 200–500 words give the algorithm more context while remaining readable. The first 2–3 lines appear above the “Show more” fold.

Hashtags & tags

YouTube allows up to 15 hashtags in the description (3 appear above the title). Use the 20 generated hashtags and pick the most relevant ones.

Keywords in tags

Paste the SEO keywords into the YouTube Tags field (comma-separated). Focus on phrases people actually search for.

Edit before publishing

Always review and customize the output. Add your real social links, affiliate disclaimers, and any specific claims that match the actual video content.
