Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are & Data Fiduciary Information

PooreYouTuber is an independent, open-access digital utility and web tool platform. We build and host specialized online calculators, text tools, and technical utilities designed to process user inputs efficiently. For the purpose of applicable data protection regulations, PooreYouTuber acts as the Data Fiduciary regarding any personal information collected directly through this website.

2. Technical Architecture & Browser-Side Processing

PooreYouTuber does not require users to create an account or profile for ordinary use of its tools. Most utility tools offered across this domain execute scripts, perform text transformations, and carry out mathematical operations locally within the user's web browser session.

However, tool architectures may vary depending on functional requirements. While primary computations are designed to execute client-side, certain dynamic integrations, API connections, or external services may involve standard client-server request dynamics. Personal data may nevertheless be processed where a user voluntarily submits information through contact/support features or where third-party service providers process data independently.

3. What Personal Data We Collect

We limit direct data collection to minimal operational necessities:

  • Voluntarily Submitted Support Information: Name (if provided), email address, and message contents submitted through our official contact channels or helpdesk forms.
  • Technical & Usage Identifiers: IP addresses, browser types, device categories, operating systems, referring URLs, and interaction timestamps collected automatically via third-party analytics and performance monitoring systems.
  • Cookies & Identifiers: Session state markers, analytical measurement cookies, and advertising preferences handled via integrated third-party partners.

4. What We Do NOT Collect

To ensure data minimization and reduce security risks:

  • We do NOT mandate user registration, accounts, or user profiles.
  • We do NOT request or store passwords or sensitive login credentials.
  • We do NOT collect, store, or process payment card details, financial accounts, or billing data.
  • We do NOT require personal personal details for ordinary usage of our tools.

5. Purpose of Processing & Legal Basis

We process personal and operational data based on legitimate business interests and user consent where required by law:

  • To Respond to Inquiries: Processing contact forms to provide technical help, resolve issues, or address user feedback.
  • Service Maintenance & Security: Monitoring platform performance, protecting against automated abuse, detecting malicious traffic, and preventing DDoS attacks.
  • Analytics & Optimization: Understanding site navigation trends, tool popularity, and technical performance to improve user experience.
  • Monetization & Advertising: Displaying non-intrusive and personalized advertisements through third-party ad networks to keep our utilities free for public access.

6. Third-Party Services Integration

We integrate trusted third-party technology providers to host, measure, and monetize our platform. Each third-party vendor operates under its own independent privacy standards:

  • Google Analytics: Used to aggregate traffic patterns, measure tool usage, and assess page performance.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store preferences and functional information. PooreYouTuber uses:

  • Analytics Cookies: Utilized by traffic monitoring systems to analyze operational performance.
  • Advertising Cookies: Set by advertising partners to deliver personalized ad experiences.

You can modify your browser settings to decline or clear cookies at any time. However, declining essential cookies may impact certain functionality or tool preferences.
