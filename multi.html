How to Use This Free Online Song Editor – Complete Guide & Benefits

Welcome to the most complete free online Song Editor available right in your browser. Whether you want to turn a regular track into immersive 8D audio, create a chilled Lofi version, make a Slowed + Reverb remix, boost the bass for car speakers, change the pitch for nightcore or deep-voice edits, or simply trim and fade your song — this tool does it all without uploading your files to any server. Everything runs locally on your device using the Web Audio API, so your music stays 100% private.

Step-by-Step: How to Use the Song Editor

1. Upload your audio or video file
Click the upload zone or drag and drop any supported file (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, FLAC, MP4, WebM, etc.). The tool will extract the audio track even from video files. Once loaded, the player and all effect controls appear.

2. Preview the original track
Use the big play button to listen to the original song. You can scrub the progress bar, adjust volume, and check the duration. This helps you decide which section to edit.

3. Choose and combine effects
Enable the effects you want using the toggles and sliders:

  • Spatial / 8D Audio – Creates the famous left-right moving surround effect popular on YouTube and TikTok. Adjust pan speed and depth. Presets: Slow 8D, Fast 8D, 3D Wide.
  • Lofi Effect – Softens high frequencies and adds gentle vinyl-style noise for that warm, nostalgic Lofi sound. Perfect for study or chill playlists.
  • Speed & Pitch – Slow the song down (Slowed) or speed it up for Nightcore. Change pitch independently to create chipmunk or deep-voice effects without changing tempo too drastically.
  • Bass Boost & EQ – Boost low frequencies for punchy bass, lift mids for vocals, or brighten treble. One-click presets for Bass Boost, Vocal Boost, and Flat EQ.
  • Reverb / Echo – Add spacious hall or dreamy reverb. Combine with Slowed for the classic “Slowed + Reverb” style.
  • Trim, Fade & Volume – Set start and end points to cut intros/outros, add smooth fade-in and fade-out, boost overall volume, or reverse the entire track.

4. Apply Effects & Preview
Click “Apply Effects & Preview”. The tool processes the audio with your chosen settings and plays the result. You can keep tweaking and re-applying until it sounds perfect.

5. Export your creation
When you are happy, click “Export WAV” for highest quality (lossless) or “Export MP3 (via WAV)” which downloads a WAV file you can easily convert to MP3 using any free converter if needed. All processing happens in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Tips for Best Results

  • For classic Slowed + Reverb: set Speed to ~0.80–0.85, enable Reverb with medium room size and wet level, and optionally add a little Lofi warmth.
  • For Nightcore: set Speed to 1.25–1.40 and raise Pitch by +3 to +5 semitones.
  • For strong 8D effect: enable Spatial, set pan speed around 0.25–0.40 and depth to maximum. Listen with headphones for the full immersive experience.
  • Always use headphones when judging 8D or spatial effects — the movement is much clearer.
  • Export as WAV for highest quality, then convert to MP3 only if you need a smaller file size.
  • If the audio becomes too loud or distorted after heavy bass boost, lower the Master Volume or reduce the bass gain slightly.
