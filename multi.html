Free AI Text Detector – Check if Content is AI-Generated or Human-Written

With the rapid rise of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other large language models, it has become increasingly difficult to tell whether a piece of writing was created by a human or by artificial intelligence. Teachers, editors, recruiters, and content managers now need reliable ways to check the origin of text. Our free AI Text Detector helps you do exactly that — quickly, privately, and without any registration.

This tool analyzes writing patterns that are statistically more common in AI-generated content. It examines sentence structure, vocabulary diversity, predictability, burstiness, and the presence of typical AI transitional phrases. The result is an overall probability score along with a clear breakdown of the signals it found.

Why Detect AI-Generated Text?

There are many practical reasons to check whether text was written by AI:

  • Education: Teachers and professors want to verify that student essays and assignments are original human work.
  • Content & SEO: Website owners and publishers prefer authentic human writing for better engagement and search rankings.
  • Hiring & Recruitment: Recruiters may want to confirm that cover letters and application materials were written by the candidate.
  • Journalism & Publishing: Editors need to maintain quality standards and originality.
  • Academic Integrity: Institutions enforce policies against undisclosed AI assistance.

How This AI Text Detector Works

The tool evaluates several linguistic and statistical features of the submitted text:

  • Predictability / Uniformity: AI models often produce very even, “safe” sentence structures.
  • Burstiness: Human writing tends to have more variation in sentence length and complexity.
  • Vocabulary Diversity: The range of unique words used relative to total word count.
  • Common AI Phrases: Presence of frequently used transitional expressions that large language models favor.
  • Repetition Patterns: Overuse of similar sentence openings or structures.
  • Average Sentence Length & Complexity: Statistical patterns that differ between human and AI writing.

These signals are combined into a single AI probability score. A higher score means the text shows more characteristics commonly associated with AI-generated content.

How to Use the Tool

  1. Paste the text you want to check into the input box (at least 50–100 characters recommended for meaningful results).
  2. Click the Analyze Text button.
  3. Review the overall AI probability score and the detailed metrics breakdown.
  4. Use the verdict as guidance — very high or very low scores are stronger indicators.

Important Limitations

No AI detector is 100% accurate. Advanced models continue to improve and can produce text that closely resembles human writing. Short texts, heavily edited AI content, or writing that deliberately mimics AI patterns can lead to less reliable results. Always use the score as one signal among others, not as absolute proof.

For best results, analyze longer samples (200+ words) when possible. Extremely short paragraphs give less statistical information and may produce less confident scores.

Privacy & Safety

This detector runs completely in your browser. The text you paste is never uploaded to any server. All calculations happen locally on your device, so your content remains private. This makes the tool suitable for sensitive documents, student work, unpublished manuscripts, and confidential material.
