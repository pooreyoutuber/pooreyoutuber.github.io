Free AI Text Detector + Grammar & Spelling Checker – Complete Guide

In today’s world of AI writing tools, it is more important than ever to understand the quality and origin of text. Our upgraded free tool combines three powerful features in one place: AI content detection, spelling & grammar checking, and a professional rewrite recommendation.

Whether you are a student, teacher, content creator, or professional, this tool helps you quickly evaluate any piece of writing. All analysis happens privately inside your browser — your text is never uploaded to any server.

What This Tool Does

  • AI Text Detection: Estimates the probability that the text was generated by artificial intelligence using linguistic patterns, predictability, and common AI phrases.
  • Spelling Checker: Finds common misspelled English words, highlights them, and suggests the correct spelling.
  • Professional Rewrite: Automatically improves your text to make it clearer, more formal, and more professional.
  • Text Statistics: Shows word count, sentence count, average sentence length, vocabulary diversity, and readability signals.

How to Use the Tool

  1. Paste your text into the input box (longer text gives more reliable results).
  2. Click Analyze Text.
  3. Review the AI probability score and detailed metrics.
  4. Check the spelling section for highlighted mistakes and suggested corrections.
  5. Read the professional rewrite recommendation and copy it if you like the improved version.

Why Spelling and Professional Tone Matter

Even small spelling mistakes can reduce credibility in emails, reports, websites, and academic work. A professional rewrite helps transform casual or rough writing into clear, polished language that is more suitable for business, academic, or public use.

This tool is especially useful when you want a quick second opinion before submitting or publishing any text.

Privacy

All processing is done locally in your browser. No text is sent to external servers. This makes the tool safe for personal, academic, and confidential content.

Limitations

The spelling checker focuses on common English misspellings. It is not a full professional grammar engine. The AI detector provides a useful estimate but is not 100% definitive — advanced AI models can sometimes produce very human-like text. Always use the results as guidance rather than absolute proof.
