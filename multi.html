Open, preview, and test multiple YouTube videos or Shorts simultaneously in a responsive grid layout directly in your browser.

How to Use the YouTube Multi Viewer

About This Tool: The YouTube Multi Viewer operates entirely client-side inside your browser sandbox. No user registration, accounts, or proxy server routing are required.

The YouTube Multi Viewer tool by PooreYoutuber offers a convenient way to preview multiple embedded video frames simultaneously. It is 100% free and works across desktops, tablets, and mobile browsers.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  • Step 1: Copy the URL of your YouTube Video or YouTube Shorts.
  • Step 2: Paste the link into the URL input field above.
  • Step 3: Select the preferred number of player screens (e.g., 2, 4, 8, or 12).
  • Step 4: Configure audio and autoplay settings based on your browser capabilities.
  • Step 5: Click Generate Players to render the embedded player grid.

Why Use a Browser Multi Viewer?

Content creators, web designers, and QA testers frequently need to test how YouTube embeds load and behave across varying grid sizes and viewport dimensions.
