Understanding the Mathematical Algorithm

It is important to remember that this tool relies strictly on a deterministic recreational algorithm based on standard letter frequencies—it is purely a mathematical game and not a scientific compatibility test. Here is how the magic works under the hood:

1. Letter Extraction: The system takes your raw input text, strips away non-alphabetic characters or numbers, and converts all letters into a single unified sequence.

2. Frequency Counting: Every unique letter present in the sentence is counted and sorted in alphabetical order to establish a starting number sequence (e.g., 5 6 9 8).

3. Fold-and-Sum Inward Reduction: The sequence is reduced round by round by pairing the first digit with the last digit, the second digit with the second-to-last digit, and so on.

4. Two-Digit Round Handling: If an addition yields a two-digit sum (for example, 5 + 8 = 13 and 6 + 9 = 15), that sum remains as a full number (13 15) for that specific round sequence display. For the subsequent round, it is expanded into individual digits (1 3 1 5) for further pairing until exactly two digits remain (e.g., 64, yielding 64%).
