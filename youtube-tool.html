How this YouTube description tool works

YouTube Description Studio helps you draft video descriptions faster. Enter a title, and the tool reads the topic, the likely viewer, and the purpose of the video from the words you used. It then writes ten descriptions in ten different styles, along with 20 hashtags and 20 keyword ideas that come from your title.

What runs on your device

Everything happens in your browser. The title is not sent to a server, and no account or API key is needed. The descriptions are built from writing templates that are combined with your topic, so they are a strong starting point, not a finished script. Read each one, remove anything that does not fit your video, and add your own details.

Before you publish

  • Replace the bracketed placeholders, such as your channel link and chapter timestamps, with real information.
  • Add timestamps only for chapters that exist in your video.
  • Check that each claim is accurate for your content. The tool does not know what your video contains.
  • Put the most useful information in the first lines. Viewers see only the opening lines before they click "Show more."
  • Use hashtags that describe your video. Check how YouTube displays them in YouTube Studio.

What this tool does not do

It does not guarantee rankings, views, subscribers, or recommendations. Search and recommendation results depend on many factors, including video quality, audience behavior, and competition. Treat the keyword ideas as starting points for your own research.
