How to Use ViralReel Multi-Viewer

Follow these simple steps to multi-preview short-form vertical videos on both Instagram and Facebook simultaneously:

  1. Copy the URL link of any public Instagram Reel or Facebook Video.
  2. Paste the URL link inside the designated input box above.
  3. Select the desired quantity of preview instances (Maximum 20 windows).
  4. Click Generate Views to render responsive viewports simultaneously.
