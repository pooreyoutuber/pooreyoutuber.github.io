💡 Technical Processing Information

Voice Cleaner Pro runs high-performance Web Audio API DSP nodes completely client-side in your browser window:

  • High-Pass & Low-Pass Filtering: Removes sub-bass electrical hum, air conditioner rumble (<80Hz), and high-frequency digital hiss (>12kHz).
  • Voice Clarity EQ: Applies a precise peaking parametric filter around 2.5kHz to elevate speech formants.
  • Spectral Downward Expander: Attenuates noise floors during quiet passages without introducing musical artifacts.
  • Offline Audio Context: Generates non-destructive sample-accurate PCM buffers for instant WAV export.
