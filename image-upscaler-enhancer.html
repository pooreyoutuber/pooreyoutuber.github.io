Auto Mode vs Manual Mode

Auto Mode is perfect when you want fast, high-quality results without adjusting anything. The tool analyzes the image and applies a carefully balanced combination of upscaling, sharpening, contrast, clarity and subtle color improvements automatically.

Manual Mode is for users who want complete control. You can independently adjust:

  • Scale – Enlarge the image by 2×, 3× or 4×
  • Brightness – Make the image lighter or darker
  • Contrast – Increase or decrease the difference between light and dark areas
  • Sharpness – Make edges and details clearer
  • Saturation – Boost or reduce overall color intensity
  • Shadows – Lift or darken the darker parts of the image
  • Highlights – Recover or tone down bright areas
  • Clarity – Add midtone contrast for more depth and definition
  • Vibrance – Smartly enhance muted colors without oversaturating skin tones

How to Use the Tool

  3. In Manual Mode, adjust the sliders and scale according to your preference.

Tips for Best Results

  • Start with Auto Mode first. If you like the result, download it immediately.
  • Use 2× scale for most everyday photos — it offers the best quality-to-size balance.
  • Increase Sharpness and Clarity moderately. Too much can create unnatural edges.
  • Lift Shadows slightly on dark photos and reduce Highlights on bright ones for better balance.
  • Use Vibrance instead of high Saturation when working with portraits to keep skin tones natural.
  • After downloading, you can further edit the image in free tools like Photopea or Canva if needed.
