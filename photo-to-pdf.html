How to Convert Photos to PDF

Combining multiple photos, receipts, or notes into a single portable document makes sharing, printing, and storing files much simpler. Our online Photo to PDF Converter allows you to select images from your phone or computer, reorder pages, adjust their orientation, crop unnecessary details, and generate a compiled PDF document instantly.

Step-by-Step Conversion Guide

  • Step 1: Upload Your Images: Click the upload box or drag and drop your photo files (JPG, PNG, or WEBP) into the upload area.
  • Step 2: Edit and Reorder: Use the built-in editor to crop with preset aspect ratios, rotate scans, or apply document enhancement filters — then drag cards to reorder pages.
  • Step 3: Choose Output Settings: Pick a page size (A4, Letter, or Fit to Image) and set the output quality to balance clarity and file size.
  • Step 4: Generate PDF: Click "Download Merged PDF" to compile your edited photos into a standardized PDF file ready for download.

Key Converter Features

✨ Image Adjustments & Crop

Refine photos before merging. Choose from aspect ratio presets (Free, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 3:2), rotate misplaced document scans, crop unwanted borders, or apply a black-and-white filter to improve text legibility.

🔀 Drag & Drop Reordering

Simply drag the photo cards into the order you want your PDF pages to appear — the page number badge updates automatically.

📐 Page Size & Quality Control

Generate your PDF in A4, Letter, or a size that exactly fits each image, and fine-tune output quality with a compression slider to control file size.

🌙 Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark themes for comfortable editing in any lighting condition.

🔒 Local Browser Processing

Your photos are processed locally inside your web browser during the conversion process rather than being stored on an external upload server.
